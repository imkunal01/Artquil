Artquil Private Limited is an AI company building a prompt-to-video generation platform. Users describe the video they want in plain language, and Artquil produces a finished video complete with synchronized audio — voiceover, background music, and sound effects — through a single automated pipeline.

Video production has traditionally been slow and expensive, requiring separate stages of scripting, filming, editing, and sound work, often involving different specialists at each step. Artquil compresses all of that into one step, making professional-quality video accessible to teams that don't have a dedicated production budget or crew.

The platform is built to serve a range of industries. Marketing and advertising teams use it to produce campaign creative at scale. E-learning providers use it to generate course content. E-commerce brands use it to create product videos. Media and entertainment studios use it to prototype concepts quickly. Enterprises use it to produce internal training material.

Behind the platform, Artquil runs on AWS, using GPU compute for model inference and cloud infrastructure to handle scalable rendering and delivery.
